# Glossary: Agricultural Biotech AI Terminology

## Terms A–M

**AMMI (Additive Main effects and Multiplicative Interaction)** – Classical statistical model decomposing multi-environment trial data into genotype/environment main effects plus a multiplicative GxE interaction term.

**APHIS (Animal and Plant Health Inspection Service)** – USDA agency with primary jurisdiction over plant pest risk regulation of biotech crops.

**Doubled haploid** – Breeding technology producing fully homozygous lines in a single generation, accelerating breeding cycle time.

**Envirotyping** – Systematic quantitative characterization of growing environments (weather, soil, management) analogous to genotyping, enabling environment-aware genomic prediction.

**Ecoregion / Agroecological zone** – Geographic region characterized by similar climate/soil/growing conditions, relevant unit for GxE and target population of environments analysis.

**FIFRA (Federal Insecticide, Fungicide, and Rodenticide Act)** – US law under which EPA regulates plant-incorporated protectants.

**GBLUP (Genomic Best Linear Unbiased Prediction)** – Standard genomic prediction method using a genomic relationship matrix within a mixed linear model framework.

**GCA/SCA (General/Specific Combining Ability)** – Quantitative genetics concepts distinguishing a parental line's average hybrid performance (GCA) from a specific parental combination's performance beyond GCA expectation (SCA).

**GEBV (Genomic Estimated Breeding Value)** – Predicted genetic value of an individual based on genome-wide marker data.

**Genomic selection** – Breeding strategy using genome-wide marker data and genomic prediction models to select breeding candidates, rather than relying solely on phenotypic evaluation.

**GxE (Genotype-by-Environment interaction)** – Phenomenon where genotype performance ranking changes across different growing environments.

**Heterosis (hybrid vigor)** – Superior performance of F1 hybrid offspring relative to their parental lines.

**Heterotic pool** – Genetically distinct breeding population maintained specifically for crossing with a complementary pool to produce superior hybrids.

**Homoeolog** – Corresponding gene copy across different sub-genomes in a polyploid species.

**LiDAR (Light Detection and Ranging)** – Laser-based distance-measurement sensing technology used for precise 3D plant structural phenotyping.

**Multi-environment trial (MET)** – Field trial network testing genotypes across multiple locations/years to characterize performance and GxE.

## Terms N–Z

**NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index)** – Common multispectral vegetation index correlating with plant biomass/health/chlorophyll content.

**Pan-genome** – Representation of genetic diversity across multiple genome accessions of a species, capturing structural variation absent from a single linear reference genome.

**Plant-incorporated protectant (PIP)** – Genetically engineered trait causing a plant to produce a pest/pathogen-control substance, regulated by EPA under pesticide law.

**Ploidy** – Number of complete chromosome sets in a cell (e.g., diploid=2, tetraploid=4, hexaploid=6); many crops are polyploid.

**QTL (Quantitative Trait Locus)** – Genomic region statistically associated with variation in a quantitative trait.

**Reaction norm model** – Statistical model representing genotype performance as a function of environmental covariates, enabling prediction for novel environments.

**SDN-1/SDN-2/SDN-3** – Site-Directed Nuclease edit categories distinguishing gene editing outcomes by genetic change type (small indel, precise small edit, larger insertion), relevant to regulatory treatment.

**SECURE rule** – 2020 USDA-APHIS biotechnology regulation update revising criteria for which genetically engineered plants require regulatory review.

**Target population of environments (TPE)** – The defined set of environments (geographic/climatic conditions) a breeding program aims to develop varieties for.

**Vegetation index** – Quantitative metric derived from spectral imagery (e.g., NDVI) correlating with plant physiological status.
