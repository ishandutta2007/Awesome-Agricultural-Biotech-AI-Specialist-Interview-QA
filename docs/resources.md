# Resources: Further Reading & Tools for Agricultural Biotech AI Specialists

## Key Guidelines & Standards
- **USDA-APHIS Biotechnology Regulations (SECURE rule, 7 CFR Part 340)** (aphis.usda.gov)
- **EPA Plant-Incorporated Protectants regulatory framework** (epa.gov)
- **FDA Biotechnology food/feed consultation process guidance** (fda.gov)
- **Coordinated Framework for Regulation of Biotechnology** (official policy documents, whitehouse.gov/OSTP archives)

## Landmark Papers
- Meuwissen, Hayes & Goddard (2001) — "Prediction of total genetic value using genome-wide dense marker maps" (Genetics) — foundational genomic prediction paper
- VanRaden (2008) — "Efficient methods to compute genomic predictions" (Journal of Dairy Science) — foundational GBLUP methodology paper (animal breeding origin, widely adapted to plants)
- Jarquín et al. (2014) — "A reaction norm model for genomic selection using high-dimensional genomic and environmental data" (Theoretical and Applied Genetics) — foundational envirotyping/GxE genomic prediction paper
- Doudna & Charpentier (2014) — CRISPR-Cas9 foundational papers (search current literature for agricultural-specific CRISPR applications)

## Textbooks & References
- **"Principles of Plant Genetics and Breeding"** (Acquaah) — foundational plant breeding textbook
- **"Genomic Selection in Animals"** (Cole & VanRaden, eds.) — genomic prediction methodology reference (animal-focused but widely applicable methodology)
- **"Statistical Methods for Plant Breeding"** (relevant biometrics/quantitative genetics texts) — experimental design and analysis reference

## Organizations
- **CGIAR** — international agricultural research center network (cgiar.org)
- **NAPB (National Association of Plant Breeders)** — US professional society and conference
- **CSSA (Crop Science Society of America)** — professional society and journal publisher
- **ISAAA** — international service for the acquisition of agri-biotech applications, tracking global biotech crop adoption
